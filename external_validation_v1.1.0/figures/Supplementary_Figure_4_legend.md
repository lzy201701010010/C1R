# Figure legend draft — proposal only

**Proposed Supplementary Figure 4. Prespecified external support for the GP_IBD–SP02 association in GSE266546.**

Each point represents one of 18 donors contributing 19 ascending-colon endoscopic samples and 15,989 epithelial cells from patients with active Crohn disease (histology: Mild, Moderate or Severe). Axes show frozen donor-level GP_IBD scores with the SP02 non-overlap mask and SP02/INFLARE scores. The inherited SM02 within-cell percentile-rank mean method was aggregated by sample medians and equal-sample donor means; the donor contributing two samples appears once. SP02 comprised MUC6, BPIFB1, AQP5 and PGC (4/4 available); the GP_IBD mask contained 324/353 available frozen features. The line displays the frozen standardized ordinary least-squares model converted to the raw-score coordinates, without refitting. The standardized coefficient was −0.873 (95% HC3 Wald confidence interval, −1.116 to −0.630; two-sided raw P = 1.84 × 10⁻¹²). This was one prespecified relationship, with no multiplicity correction. No confidence band is shown because the frozen result lacks the full coefficient covariance needed to reconstruct it; the displayed interval is for the slope. The cohort is source-lineage independent with no documented overlap with the original C1-R cohorts; participant-level non-overlap cannot be established deterministically. The result supports a donor-level expression-score association, not causality or clinical utility.
